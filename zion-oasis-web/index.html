Our Story

More than a restaurant.
A way of life.

What started as a humble beach shack over a decade ago has evolved into Marsaskala's most beloved dining destination — ranked #1 of 85 restaurants.

Nestled along the serene shores of St. Thomas Bay, Zion is your outdoor living room by the sea. We blend Mediterranean tradition with island soul, creating a space where time slows down and every meal becomes a memory.

From our sun-kissed terrace to our tranquil olive garden, every corner of Zion is designed for one thing: making you feel at home by the water.
